Wingman

Never fantasy alone.

Your fantasy football second screen.

Fantasy football apps are great for managing your team. Wingman is for living through game day.

Think of it as RedZone for your fantasy universe: live matchups, plays, projections, and the little moments that can swing an entire week, all in one visual, interactive companion.

Your leagues stay where they are. Wingman rides shotgun.

What's inside

- Matchups — Follow multiple leagues with live scoring, projections, win probabilities, and scoring alerts.
- Field — See your players' runs, catches, and throws on a live football field, not just in a stat line.
- Survivor & Pick'em — Track picks, results, and what's at stake as games unfold.
- Roster Lab — Explore roster strength, potential pickups, and trades without leaving the game-day view.

The vision

The goal isn't another fantasy platform or a wall of numbers. It's the companion you keep open next to the game and the apps you already use.

Wingman should connect leagues across platforms, respect each league's scoring rules, and turn live NFL events into clear, meaningful signals. Show what matters. Let the details live one hover away.

No roster changes. No replacing your league app. Just a better way to experience everything happening around it.

Where it's at

Wingman is actively evolving. Sleeper is the current foundation; broader integrations, including Yahoo, ESPN, MyFantasyLeague, and Fleaflicker, are being developed. Availability varies by provider and API access.
